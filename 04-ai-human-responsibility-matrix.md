# 04. AI vs. Human Responsibility Matrix

This matrix clearly delineates what tasks are fully automated by AI, what requires human execution, and what requires human approval during our 90-day sprint and future salesperson handover.

| Process / Task Area | Delegated to AI (Autonomous) | Human Execution (Owner/Salesperson) | Human Approval Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Inquiry & Scheduling** | Automated booking links, instant calendar invites, and pre-consult confirmation emails. | Reviewing daily calendar prep and prospect background profiles. | N/A |
| **Consultation Call (30 min)** | Transcribing call notes, summarizing financial pain points (via AI tooling). | Conducting the diagnostic conversation, pitching services, anchoring price, and offering 24-hr discount. | N/A |
| **24-Hour Discount Offer** | Triggering the automated 24-hour expiration reminder email/SMS post-call. | Customizing custom scoping or special contract terms if requested on call. | Approving custom discounts beyond standard 30%. |
| **7-Touch Follow-Up Cadence** | Drafting personalized emails, sending automated touches 1–7, updating GHL pipeline stages. | Reviewing replies, handling complex tax/accounting objections over phone or email. | Approving any aggressive outreach exceptions or custom proposals. |
| **Pipeline & Status Tracking** | Auto-tagging contacts, incrementing `Follow-Up Step`, updating Kanban cards. | Reviewing daily Smart Lists and auditing pipeline hygiene weekly. | Marking opportunities as `Closed-Won` or `Closed-Lost`. |
