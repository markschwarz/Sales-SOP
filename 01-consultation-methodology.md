# 01. Consultation Methodology

## Overview
The WellnessFi initial consultation is a strategic 30-minute diagnostic call tailored for private practice owners and therapists. 

## Key Scientific & Behavioral Principles
1. **Commitment & Consistency (Cialdini):** People strive to honor verbal commitments they articulate. Moving from 20 to 30 minutes ensures there is dedicated closing time to secure commitments while momentum is highest.
2. **Price Anchoring & Loss Aversion (Kahneman & Tversky):** Setting a standard upfront onboarding fee establishes professional value, followed by a time-sensitive 30% discount expiring in exactly 24 hours to trigger loss aversion.

## Call Structure (30 Minutes)
* **Minutes 0–15: Diagnosis & Discovery**
  * Uncover current practice financials (bookkeeping pain points, tax setup, entity structure / S-Corp considerations).
  * Identify specific pain points (e.g., missed deadlines, CPA turnover, tax overpayments).
* **Minutes 15–20: Value Alignment & Solution Mapping**
  * Present the recommended WellnessFi service tier (Foundations, Full Service, or CFO).
  * Connect our expertise directly to their practice growth and compliance goals.
* **Minutes 20–30: The Closing Window & Commitment**
  * Outline the standard onboarding setup investment (anchored at full price).
  * Introduce the **24-Hour Fast-Action Incentive**: A 30% discount on the setup fee if signed within 24 hours of call completion.
  * Secure a firm verbal agreement or schedule a definitive follow-up commitment before hanging up.
