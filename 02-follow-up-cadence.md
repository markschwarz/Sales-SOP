# 02. The 7-Touch Post-Consultation Follow-Up Cadence

## Research Rationale
Industry sales benchmarks show that ~80% of sales require 5+ follow-up touches, yet 44% of reps stop after one. For warm post-consultation therapy practice owners, a structured 7-touch cadence combining value-add insights and direct check-ins doubles conversion rates without feeling pushy.

## The 7-Touch Sequence

* **Touch 1 (Day 2): Value + Recap**
  * *Channel:* Email + SMS
  * *Content:* Thank-you recap of the consult + links to the specific resource or financial tool discussed.
* **Touch 2 (Day 6): Value Insight**
  * *Channel:* Email
  * *Content:* A quick tip or relevant tax/accounting update tailored to private practices (e.g., S-Corp savings insights).
* **Touch 3 (Day 11): Social Proof / Case Study**
  * *Channel:* Email
  * *Content:* How WellnessFi helped a similar practice owner solve this exact accounting bottleneck.
* **Touch 4 (Day 18): Direct Check-In**
  * *Channel:* SMS / Email
  * *Content:* Short and punchy: *"Hey [Name], checking in—did you decide on your accounting setup for this quarter?"*
* **Touch 5 (Day 26): Light Value Checklist**
  * *Channel:* Email
  * *Content:* A concise financial planning checklist or year-end milestone reminder.
* **Touch 6 (Day 35): The Direct "Break-Up" Touch**
  * *Channel:* Email
  * *Content:* *"Are you still looking for support, or should I take you off my active list?"* (Triggers high response rates due to loss aversion).
* **Touch 7 (Day 50): Seasonal Trigger**
  * *Channel:* Email / SMS
  * *Content:* Final check-in tied to an upcoming tax deadline or quarterly filing milestone.
